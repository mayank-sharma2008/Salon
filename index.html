Our Story

Where Craft Meets
Conviction

AURUM was born from a simple belief: that beauty is not a service — it's an art form. Founded in New Delhi, our atelier brings together master stylists, colorists, and wellness artisans under one roof, united by an obsession with craft and a reverence for the individual.

Every treatment begins with a conversation. Every style is designed to amplify not just how you look, but how you feel. We don't follow trends — we interpret them through the lens of your unique presence.
